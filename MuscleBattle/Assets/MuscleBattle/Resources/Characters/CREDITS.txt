MUSCLE BATTLE 2.0 character assets

- Fighter bodies, faces, eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes, hair (short02 / ponytail01) and skin textures
  (young_asian_male / young_asian_female): MakeHuman / MPFB2 system assets, CC0 1.0.
  https://static.makehumancommunity.org  (makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip)
  Bodies shaped with MakeHuman targets in MPFB2 (Blender add-on, GPLv3 code; generated output is ours),
  Mixamo-compatible "mixamo_unity" rig in T-pose.
- Outfits (Jin: sleeveless vest, pants, belt, gloves / Ray: crop top, pants, gloves, arm bands) are modelled
  from the body surface in Blender by tools/character_build/mpfb/build_mpfb.py and rigged with copied weights.
  Textures baked by the same script. Original work for this project.
- Animations: Quaternius "Universal Animation Library" 1 & 2 (Standard), CC0. Retargeted through Unity Humanoid.
https://quaternius.com
